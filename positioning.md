# Positioning — The Hub (working name)

> A value-first editorial AI content hub. A library, not a storefront. The only
> "pitch" is a soft door into a free community, discovered after the reader gets
> value. This file is the source of truth for what the site is and who it is for.
> (Placeholder name lives in `lib/site.ts` — renaming is a one-line edit.)

## The one idea

**Learn to actually use AI to make money and make things, without the hype.**

It encodes three vectors (use it, earn with it, create with it) plus the
differentiator: practical, no-hype, for normal people. Swappable phrasings of
the same idea: "AI you can actually use to earn and create." / "The practical
side of AI: make money, make things, skip the noise."

## Who it is for (ICP)

The curious-but-overwhelmed beginner-to-intermediate. Creators, side-hustlers,
and 9-to-5ers who want a second income or a creative outlet. They have poked at
a chat tool or an image tool and suspect there is money and creative upside in
AI. They want to *do* something and see a result.

They are NOT engineers, agent developers, or people who want to read about model
architectures. Every page, title, and article reads like a plain-language answer
to what they actually search:
- "how to make money with AI", "best AI tools for beginners", "faceless AI channel"
- "make money with AI voices", "AI side hustle that actually works"
- "how to create [videos/art/a channel] with AI", "is [tool] worth it / A vs B"

Frustrations we solve: tool overload, hype with no path, guru noise, no starting
point. Every piece ends at a real result.

## Content pillars (the nav + content spine — a learning sequence, 01 → 04)

1. **Use AI** — practical foundations. Where to start, which tools matter, how to
   get a good result first try. Kills tool overload.
2. **Make Money with AI** — the income paths, walked through end to end. Highest
   commercial-intent surface.
3. **Create with AI** — the maker side. Video, art, characters, music, writing.
   Most shareable content.
4. **Tools & Workflows** — the stack, reviewed honestly, and how pieces wire up.
   The most evergreen SEO surface.

Every article gets exactly one pillar. Pillars power the index filter, Start
Here, and `/articles/topic/[pillar]`.

## The single soft CTA (the only pitch on the whole site)

> It's all free to read here. When you want to go deeper with people building the
> same thing, the community's open.

Value earns the right to invite. The CTA is a continuation ("where this goes
deeper"), never a conversion ("SIGN UP NOW"). One quiet persistent nav link plus
a few earned end-of-value moments. No number does the persuading. The one honest
claim allowed: **it's free to join** (the community's free tier is live).

## Conversion model

The inverse of a sales site. Test for every CTA placement: "would this feel
welcome to someone who just learned something from us?" If no, it does not ship.
No page except `/community` renders more than one dedicated community block.
