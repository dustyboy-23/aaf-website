Every local business, every e-commerce brand, every personal brand needs content. Even if you charge on the lower end, you're making $200+/hour effective rate.</p><h2 id="phase-1-setup-0-%E2%80%94-week-1-2">Phase 1: Setup ($0 — Week 1-2)</h2><h3 id="your-ai-toolkit-start-free">Your AI Toolkit (Start Free)</h3><p>You don't need to spend money to start. Here's your free stack:</p><ul><li><strong>ChatGPT free tier:</strong> For initial content generation and brainstorming</li><li><strong>Canva free tier:</strong> For graphics and simple video editing</li><li><strong>Google Docs:</strong> For content delivery and collaboration</li><li><strong>Buffer free tier:</strong> For scheduling up to 3 social channels</li><li><strong>Your own social media:</strong> Your portfolio is your own content</li></ul><p>Total investment: $0. You can upgrade tools as revenue comes in.</p><h3 id="pick-your-niche">Pick Your Niche</h3><p>Don't try to serve everyone. The riches are in niches. Good niches for AI content agencies:</p><ul><li><strong>Local businesses:</strong> Restaurants, gyms, salons, contractors (they have money, no content skills)</li><li><strong>E-commerce brands:</strong> Need constant social content and ads</li><li><strong>Real estate agents:</strong> Need listing content, market updates, and personal brand content</li><li><strong>SaaS companies:</strong> Need blog content, social proof, and educational content</li><li><strong>Personal brands / coaches:</strong> Need everything but don't want to create it themselves</li></ul><p>Pick one. Become the expert. You can expand later. I talk about this approach in my <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">sell</a> article — the principles are the same.</p><h3 id="build-your-portfolio-3-5-days">Build Your Portfolio (3-5 Days)</h3><p>No clients yet? No problem. Create sample work:</p><ul><li>Pick 2-3 businesses in your target niche (real businesses)</li><li>Create a week's worth of sample content for each</li><li>Put it in a clean Google Doc or Notion page</li><li>This is your portfolio — it shows what you can do</li></ul><h2 id="phase-2-first-clients-500-1500mo-%E2%80%94-week-3-6">Phase 2: First Clients ($500-1,500/mo — Week 3-6)</h2><h3 id="where-to-find-clients">Where to Find Clients</h3><p>In order of what's worked best for me:</p><ul><li><strong>Local outreach:</strong> Walk into businesses, show them your portfolio, explain what you do. Old school but effective. "I noticed your Instagram hasn't posted in 3 weeks — I can fix that for $500/month."</li><li><strong>Facebook groups:</strong> Join groups where business owners hang out. Be helpful. When someone asks about content, DM them.</li><li><strong>Fiverr/Upwork:</strong> Great for building reviews and getting initial clients. Price lower to start, raise prices as you get reviews.</li><li><strong>Cold email:</strong> Find businesses with bad or inactive social media. Send a personalized email with a sample post you created for them.</li><li><strong>Referrals:</strong> After your first client, ask them who else might need this. Word of mouth is the best channel.</li></ul><h3 id="how-to-price-your-services">How to Price Your Services</h3><p>Start at the lower end of the range to land your first 2-3 clients. Once you have results and testimonials, raise prices. My pricing progression:</p><ul><li><strong>Months 1-2:</strong> $300-500/mo per client</li><li><strong>Months 3-4:</strong> $500-800/mo per client (with results to show)</li><li><strong>Months 5-6:</strong> $800-1,500/mo per client (with testimonials and case studies)</li></ul><p>At 3 clients averaging $500/month, you're at $1,500/month. That's Phase 2 complete.</p><h2 id="phase-3-systems-and-scale-1500-3000mo-%E2%80%94-month-2-3">Phase 3: Systems and Scale ($1,500-3,000/mo — Month 2-3)</h2><p>Once you have a few clients, you need systems. This is where most people get stuck — they do everything manually and burn out at 4-5 clients.</p><h3 id="build-your-content-machine">Build Your Content Machine</h3><ul><li><strong>Templatize everything:</strong> Create templates for each content type in each niche</li><li><strong>Upgrade your AI tools:</strong> Now is the time to invest in ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, or both ($20-40/month)</li><li><strong>Create brand voice profiles:</strong> Detailed docs for each client that you feed to AI every session</li><li><strong>Set up scheduling:</strong> Paid Buffer or Publer plan for multi-client management</li><li><strong>Create SOPs:</strong> Document every process so it's repeatable</li></ul><h3 id="the-ai-content-workflow-that-scales">The AI Content Workflow That Scales</h3><p>Here's my actual workflow per client per month:</p><ul><li><strong>Day 1:</strong> AI generates content calendar based on brand voice doc (30 min)</li><li><strong>Day 1:</strong> I review and customize the calendar (30 min)</li><li><strong>Day 2-3:</strong> AI generates all content (60 min total)</li><li><strong>Day 3:</strong> I review and edit everything (60 min)</li><li><strong>Day 3:</strong> Schedule all content for the month (30 min)</li><li><strong>Ongoing:</strong> Monitor and adjust based on performance (30 min/week)</li></ul><p>Total: roughly 5-6 hours per client per month. At $800/client, that's $130+/hour.</p><p>For the tech-minded, you can use <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">agents</a> to automate even more of this pipeline.
