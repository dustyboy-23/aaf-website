<p>I get this question every single day in our Skool community. "Is ChatGPT an AI agent?" "Is Siri an AI agent?" "What makes something an agent vs just an assistant?"</p><p>The confusion is real. Just what each actually does and when you should use which.</p><h2 id="the-simple-definition">The Simple Definition</h2><p>An <strong>AI assistant</strong> answers questions and follows instructions one at a time. You ask, it responds. You ask again, it responds again. It waits for you.</p><p>An <strong>AI agent</strong> takes a goal and works toward it autonomously. You give it an objective. It plans, executes, handles errors, and loops until the job is done. It does not wait for you between steps.</p><p>That is the core difference. Reactive vs proactive. <h2 id="real-world-examples-that-make-it-click">Real World Examples That Make It Click</h2><h3 id="writing-a-blog-post">Writing a Blog Post</h3><p><strong>AI Assistant approach:</strong> You prompt ChatGPT. "Write a blog post about AI agents." It gives you a draft. You read it, ask for edits. You copy it to WordPress. You format it. You add images. You publish it. 45 minutes of your time.</p><p><strong>AI Agent approach:</strong> You tell your agent "Write and publish a 2,000-word blog post about AI agents targeting the keyword 'ai agents for business.' Add internal links, a FAQ section, and publish it on Ghost." It writes, formats, links, and publishes while you do something else. 0 minutes of your time after the initial prompt.</p><h3 id="managing-customer-emails">Managing Customer Emails</h3><p><strong>AI Assistant approach:</strong> You paste an email into Claude. "Draft a reply." It gives you a reply. You paste the next email. Repeat 50 times.</p><p><strong>AI Agent approach:</strong> Your agent monitors your inbox. It categorizes emails. Drafts replies for routine ones. Flags urgent ones. Sends follow-ups after 48 hours of no response. You review a summary once a day.</p><h3 id="research">Research</h3><p><strong>AI Assistant approach:</strong> "What are the top CRM tools?" You get a list. You ask follow-ups one by one.</p><p><strong>AI Agent approach:</strong> "Research the top 10 CRM tools, compare pricing, features, and integrations with our tech stack. Create a spreadsheet with a recommendation." You get a finished deliverable.</p><h2 id="the-spectrum-between-assistant-and-agent">The Spectrum Between Assistant and Agent</h2><p>It is not actually binary. There is a spectrum.
