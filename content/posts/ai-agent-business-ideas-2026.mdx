<h2 id="1-ai-content-agency">1. AI Content Agency</h2><p>This is the business I know best because I run one. You produce content (blogs, social media, email, video scripts) for clients using AI agents. The key: you are not selling "AI content." You are selling results. Rankings. Traffic. Engagement.</p><p>I covered the full roadmap in my <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">agency</a> guide, but here is the quick version:</p><ul><li><strong>What you deliver:</strong> 20-50 blog posts per month, social media content calendars, email sequences</li><li><strong>What you charge:</strong> $1,500-5,000 per client per month</li><li><strong>Your cost per client:</strong> $50-150 in AI API costs + 5-10 hours of oversight</li><li><strong>How to get clients:</strong> Cold outreach to businesses with bad content, referrals from existing clients</li></ul><p>The margin is insane. A client paying $3,000/month costs me about $100 in AI + 8 hours of work. That is $362/hour effective rate. You can see how I produce volume in my <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">90-posts</a> breakdown.</p><h2 id="2-ai-agent-setup-service">2. AI Agent Setup Service</h2><p>Businesses know they need AI agents but have no idea how to set them up. You do it for them. One-time setup fees of $500-2,000 plus optional monthly maintenance of $200-500.</p><p>I detailed this in my <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">sell-setups</a> article. The typical engagement looks like:</p><ul><li>Audit the client's workflow (1-2 hours)</li><li>Identify 3-5 automatable processes</li><li>Build the agent pipeline (4-8 hours)</li><li>Document and hand off</li></ul><p>Your clients are typically small business owners, real estate agents, coaches, and e-commerce operators. They do not want to learn the tech. They want it done for them. <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">biz-owners</a> breaks down exactly what they are willing to spend.</p><h2 id="3-niche-saas-with-ai-agents">3. Niche SaaS With AI Agents</h2><p>This is the highest ceiling play. Build a software product that uses AI agents to solve a specific problem for a specific market. The barrier to entry is higher because you need to build and maintain software. But the recurring revenue and scalability are unmatched.</p><p>Examples that are working right now:</p><ul><li>AI property description generator for real estate (charges per listing)</li><li>AI customer support agent for e-commerce (monthly subscription)</li><li>AI bookkeeper that categorizes transactions (monthly subscription)</li><li>AI social media manager for restaurants (monthly subscription)</li></ul><p>The key to niche SaaS is to go narrow. Not "AI for business." That is a death sentence. "AI that writes property listings for Realtors in Texas." That is a business. I talked about this in <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">niche</a>.</p><h2 id="4-ai-powered-lead-generation">4. AI-Powered Lead Generation</h2><p>Use AI agents to find, qualify, and reach out to leads for clients. This works especially well for B2B services, real estate, and professional services.</p><p>The agent pipeline:</p><ol><li>Scrape relevant directories, LinkedIn, or public databases for prospects</li><li>Use AI to score and qualify leads based on client criteria</li><li>Generate personalized outreach (email, LinkedIn messages)</li><li>Track responses and hand off warm leads to the client</li></ol><p>Charge per qualified lead ($25-100) or a monthly retainer ($1,500-5,000). Clients care about results, not your process. When leads turn into deals worth $10,000+, a $50 cost per lead is nothing. You can automate the outreach portion with the techniques I describe in <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">social</a>.</p><h2 id="5-ai-agent-training-and-courses">5. AI Agent Training and Courses</h2><p>If you have successfully built AI agents, other people will pay you to teach them.
