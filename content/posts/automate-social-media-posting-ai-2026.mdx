It writes captions, adapts content for each platform, suggests optimal posting times based on your audience data, and even generates images or short-form video clips.</p><p>I run content across 6+ platforms for multiple brands. Compare that to hiring even a part-time social media manager at $1,500+/month, and the math is obvious.</p><h2 id="step-1-build-your-content-calendar-with-ai">Step 1: Build Your Content Calendar With AI</h2><p>Everything starts with a content calendar. I used to build these manually in Notion — now I have AI generate the first draft.</p><p>Here's my process:</p><ul><li>Feed AI your brand voice doc, target audience, and content pillars</li><li>Ask it to generate 30 days of content ideas across all platforms</li><li>Review, cut the weak ones, and add your own ideas</li><li>Organize by platform and content type</li></ul><p>The key insight: AI is great at volume but mediocre at knowing what's <em>actually</em> trending in your niche. Use it for the framework, then inject your own real-world observations.</p><h3 id="prompt-template-i-use">Prompt Template I Use</h3><p>I keep this in a custom GPT, but here's the gist:</p><p><em>"You are a social media strategist for [brand]. Target audience: [details]. Content pillars: [list]. Generate a 30-day content calendar with daily posts for TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook. Each post needs: hook, caption, CTA, and 5 hashtags. Mix formats: 40% short-form video concepts, 30% carousel/image posts, 20% text/story posts, 10% engagement posts."</em></p><h2 id="step-2-generate-content-in-batches">Step 2: Generate Content in Batches</h2><p>Don't create content one post at a time. Batch it. I sit down once a week and generate all the written content for the next 7-10 days.</p><p>For video scripts, I use a workflow where AI generates first drafts based on my content calendar, I review and edit the hooks (hooks are everything — I'm very picky about these), and then pass them to my editors or record them myself.</p><p>For image posts and carousels, AI generates the copy and I use Canva's AI features or dedicated image generators to create the visuals. Check out my guide on <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">money</a> for more ways to leverage this kind of output.</p><h3 id="platform-specific-adaptations">Platform-Specific Adaptations</h3><p>One piece of content should never be posted identically across platforms. Each platform has different vibes:</p><ul><li><strong>TikTok:</strong> Raw, fast, hook in first second, trending sounds</li><li><strong>Instagram Reels:</strong> Slightly more polished, strong visual hook, carousel-friendly</li><li><strong>Facebook:</strong> Longer captions okay, community-oriented, shares matter</li><li><strong>X/Twitter:</strong> Short, punchy, thread-friendly, hot takes perform</li><li><strong>LinkedIn:</strong> Professional but human, story-format, value-first</li><li><strong>YouTube Shorts:</strong> Hook-first, educational, clear CTA</li></ul><p>AI can reformat one core piece of content into all of these variations in seconds. That's the real time savings.</p><h2 id="step-3-set-up-your-scheduling-pipeline">Step 3: Set Up Your Scheduling Pipeline</h2><p>Once content is created, it needs to get scheduled. Here's my flow:</p><ul><li>Content lives in a Google Sheet or Notion database</li><li>Zapier/Make pulls approved content and sends it to Buffer or Publer</li><li>Buffer handles optimal time scheduling per platform</li><li>I get a daily digest of what's about to go out (final review chance)</li></ul><p>The approval gate is critical. I never let AI post without my review. Not because the content is bad — it's usually 80% there — but because brand voice matters and one off-brand post can undo weeks of trust building.</p><h2 id="step-4-automate-engagement-and-analytics">Step 4: Automate Engagement and Analytics</h2><p>Posting is only half the battle. Engagement and analysis are the other half.</p><p>For engagement, I use AI to draft replies to comments. Not auto-reply — I draft them, read them, and then send. This cuts my reply time by about 70%.</p><p>For analytics, I have a weekly automated report that pulls data from each platform and uses AI to identify patterns: what content types are performing, what hooks are working, what times get the best engagement.</p><p>If you want to go deeper on using <a href="https://aiagentsfirst.com/">agents</a> for this kind of analytical work, that's a whole separate topic worth exploring.</p><h2 id="step-5-scale-with-ai-agents">Step 5: Scale With AI Agents</h2><p>This is where it gets really interesting.
