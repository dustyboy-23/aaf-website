Weekly Newsletter</h3><p>Every Monday at 6 AM, my writing agent pulls three things: the latest AI news from my RSS feeds, my recent blog posts, and engagement data from the previous week. It writes a 600-word newsletter with a personal opener, 3 curated links with summaries, and a CTA.</p><p>The agent knows my voice because I gave it 20 examples of newsletters I wrote manually plus a style guide that says "write like you are talking to a friend who is curious about AI agents. No corporate speak. Short paragraphs."</p><h3 id="2-welcome-sequence-5-emails-over-10-days">2. Welcome Sequence (5 Emails Over 10 Days)</h3><p>When someone joins my list, they enter a 5-email drip sequence. These emails were written once by my agent and I approved them. They introduce me, share my best content, and make a soft pitch for the Skool community.</p><p>The open rate on email 1 is 67%. By email 5 it is still 41%. That retention tells me the content is hitting.</p><h3 id="3-product-launch-sequences">3. Product Launch Sequences</h3><p>When I launch something new, I give my agent: the product details, the target segment, and the launch timeline. It generates a 4-email sequence (announcement, value story, FAQ + objections, last chance). I review, tweak maybe 10%, and schedule.</p><h3 id="4-re-engagement-campaigns">4. Re-engagement Campaigns</h3><p>Every 30 days, my analytics agent identifies subscribers who have not opened an email in 60+ days. It generates a "miss you" email with a fresh angle or freebie. If they still do not engage after 2 re-engagement emails, they get removed from the list to keep deliverability high.</p><h2 id="how-the-writing-agent-works-technical-details">How the Writing Agent Works (Technical Details)</h2><p>The writing agent is a Python script. Here is the high-level flow:</p><ol><li>Pull context (news feeds, blog posts, performance data)</li><li>Build a prompt with the context + style guide + email type template</li><li>Call Claude Sonnet 4 API to generate the email</li><li>Run a quality check (word count, spam word detection, link validation)</li><li>Push to Kit via API as a draft or scheduled broadcast</li><li>Log the result to a tracking spreadsheet</li></ol><p>The quality check step is critical. My agent rejects emails that contain spam trigger words (like "free money" or "act now"), have broken links, or exceed 800 words. If rejected, it regenerates with adjusted instructions.</p><h2 id="results-before-vs-after-ai-agents">Results: Before vs After AI Agents</h2>
